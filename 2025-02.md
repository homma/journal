### clang-repl を試す
> date: 2025-02-09  
> topics: c++

````console
$ brew install llvm
$ alias clang-repl="$(brew --prefix llvm)/bin/clang-repl -Xcc=-std=c++23"
$ clang-repl 
clang-repl> #include <print>
clang-repl> std::println("hello");
hello
````

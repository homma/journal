### clang-repl で SDL2 を使用する
> date: 2025-02-10  
> topics: c++, sdl2

````console
$ export DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH=$(brew --prefix sdl2)/lib
$ clang-repl --Xcc=-I$(brew --prefix sdl2)/include
%lib libSDL2.dylib
#include <SDL2/SDL.h>
auto v = SDL_version{};
SDL_GetVersion(&v);
#include <print>
std::println("{}.{}.{}", v.major, v.minor, v.patch);
2.30.12
````

### clang-repl を試す
> date: 2025-02-09  
> topics: c++

````console
$ brew install llvm
$ alias clang-repl="$(brew --prefix llvm)/bin/clang-repl --Xcc=-std=c++2c"
$ clang-repl 
clang-repl> #include <print>
clang-repl> std::println("hello");
hello
````
